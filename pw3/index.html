THE DISCOVERY DESK

Three things worth knowing

01 / GEOGRAPHY

A planet of water

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. The oceans connect continents and help regulate our climate.

Explore the source at USGS

02 / SCIENCE

Sunlight takes a journey

Light from the Sun takes about eight minutes to reach Earth. Looking at sunshine means seeing light that began its journey minutes ago.

Explore the source at NASA

03 / HISTORY

Ideas on the move

Johannes Gutenberg's printing work in 15th-century Europe helped books reach a much wider audience, changing how knowledge travelled.

Explore the Gutenberg Bible
